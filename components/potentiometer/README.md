# potentiometer

Rotary / slide potentiometers for LTspice, with the taper defined numerically
(the A/B/C letters mean different curves at different makers).

Files: `potentiometer.sub` (all subckts), `pot_*.asy` (symbols, generated by
`make_symbols.py`), `bench_potentiometer.py` (verification), `demo_potentiometer.asc`.

## Pins

In the lug order printed on pot datasheets: **CCW** (lug 1, the end the wiper
reaches fully anticlockwise), **W** (lug 2, wiper), **CW** (lug 3). In the symbols CW is
on top (16,16), CCW at the bottom (16,112), W on the right (64,64) — a volume control
drawn the usual way (input on top, ground at the bottom) gets louder with `rot`.

> Differs from the older `sources/electrucio/` pots (pins `1 2 3` = end, end,
> wiper). Map: electrucio pin 1 → CCW, pin 2 → CW, pin 3 → W.

## Variants

| subckt | pins | taper |
|---|---|---|
| `pot_lin` | CCW W CW | linear, f = rot |
| `pot_log` | CCW W CW | "audio"/A: exponential through (0,0), (0.5, L), (1,1); `seg=1` for two straight segments meeting at (0.5, L) |
| `pot_revlog` | CCW W CW | reverse audio/C, mirror of `pot_log` |
| `pot_pts` | CCW W CW | any taper from three points: f10, f50, f90 = fraction of R between CCW and W at 10/50/90 % rotation, linear in between |
| `pot_sym` | CCW W CW | symmetric about the centre, f(0.25) = Lq: Lq < 0.25 S-curve, Lq > 0.25 fast at the ends ("W" at some makers) |
| `pot_dual_lin/log/revlog` | CCW1 W1 CW1 CCW2 W2 CW2 | ganged; section 2 off by `drot` (rotation) and `dR` (relative resistance): the tracking error |
| `pot_log_tap` | CCW W CW TAP | tapped track (loudness taps) at rotation `tap` |
| `pot_lin_vc/log_vc/revlog_vc` | CCW W CW CTRL | rotation = V(CTRL) in volts (0..1), for sweeps in `.tran` |

Parameters: `R` total, `rot` 0..1 (clamped; a dial at d of 10 is rot = d/10), `L`
fraction at mid-rotation for log tapers (0.10 = IEC "10A"/-20 dB, 0.15 = "15A"),
`Rend` residual resistance at each end (default 1 Ω, avoids zero-ohm topologies), `Rw`
wiper contact resistance (default 1 mΩ).

Knobs are swept with `.step param`: `X1 a w b pot_log R=500k rot={vol}` +
`.step param vol list 0 0.3 0.6 1`.

## Verification

`python3 bench_potentiometer.py` (sandbox off) — **83/83 checks pass** (2026-09-14):
every taper at rot = −0.2 (clamp), 0, 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 0.9, 1, 1.3 against the
formula (tolerance 1e-6 of the divider ratio); total resistance; the ganged sections
track (and, with drot = 0.02 and dR = +10 %, section 2 lands where the formula says —
+0.8 dB at rot = 0.2 in the example); the tapped pot below, at and above the tap; the
wiper resistance against a Thevenin calculation; the voltage-controlled pots along a
ramp; `.step param` on `rot`. The demo schematic netlists (tools/ltspice/asc2net.py)
with pins in the right order and simulates.

## Provenance

Original work for this library (Claude with Electrucio, 2026-09-14). The idea of
parameterising a log taper by its mid-travel ratio L and of S-shaped symmetric
laws comes from the IEC 60393-style library `R-poti.lib` found on LTwiki
(`sources/ltwiki/extracted/lib/lib/sub/R-poti.lib`); no text was copied. The
three-point taper and the tracking-error parameters follow the project's guide
(`docs/user/guia_componentes_audio_spice.pdf`, section 6, routes M26 in
`catalog/user/modelos.yaml`).

## Limits

No wiper noise, no contact bounce, no taper tolerance (±20 % on R is typical — sweep
`R` if it matters), no power rating. The voltage-controlled versions use B-sources
(LTspice/ngspice syntax); the static ones are plain resistors and port anywhere.
